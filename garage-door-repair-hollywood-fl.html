Common Questions

Do you provide garage door repair near me in Broward County?
Yes. We service Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Davie, Plantation, Miramar and surrounding areas.
Do you offer same-day garage door repair?
Yes. We offer same-day garage door repair across South Florida, and in many cases we can arrive within 30–90 minutes depending on location and availability.
How much does garage door repair cost?
Garage door repair costs depend on the issue. Minor repairs may cost around $100–$300, while spring replacement or opener repairs may range from $250–$600+. We always provide a clear quote before starting any work.
Do you offer free estimates?
Yes. We provide free estimates for most repairs and installations, and we explain all available options before beginning any work.
How long does a garage door repair take?
Most repairs take 30–90 minutes depending on the issue. Our technicians carry common parts so repairs can usually be completed during the first visit.
Is it safe to open a garage door with a broken spring?
No. A garage door with a broken spring can weigh 150–300 lbs. Forcing it open can damage the opener or cause injury. A professional technician should repair it.
Should I replace one spring or both?
Garage doors use two springs that work together to carry the weight of the door. If one breaks, the other is usually worn as well. For safety and proper balance we recommend replacing both springs together.
Do you service all garage door brands?
Yes. We service most major brands including LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Craftsman and more.
