Why Maintenance Matters

Garage doors weigh hundreds of pounds and operate under extreme spring tension. Broken springs, cables or off-track doors can be dangerous to repair without the proper tools and training. Our technicians safely repair all garage door systems.

  • Our technicians inspect the full door system, including the safety sensors and opener.
  • Minor adjustments and tightening are handled on the same visit.
  • Any repair found on the visit is quoted clearly before work starts.

Common Questions

Do you offer free estimates?
Yes. We provide free estimates for most repairs and installations, and we explain all available options before beginning any work.
How much does garage door repair cost?
Garage door repair costs depend on the issue. Minor repairs may cost around $100–$300, while spring replacement or opener repairs may range from $250–$600+. We always provide a clear quote before starting any work.
Do you service all garage door brands?
Yes. We service most major brands including LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Craftsman and more.
Do you offer same-day garage door repair?
Yes. We offer same-day garage door repair across South Florida, and in many cases we can arrive within 30–90 minutes depending on location and availability.
